Episode: Ep 301: Elena Verna on Growth Loops & Product-Led Growth
Guest: Elena Verna
File: ep301_elena_verna.txt

Product-Led Growth (PLG) is a business model where the product is the primary acquisition, retention, and expansion engine ("the product is the channel, the product is the sales rep"). In a true PLG motion, sustainable growth is driven by self-serve product loops, direct user value delivery, and organic expansion rather than isolated marketing hacks or sales-led playbooks. Common growth tactics fail when companies treat them as temporary hacks rather than systematic, closed compounding loops.
